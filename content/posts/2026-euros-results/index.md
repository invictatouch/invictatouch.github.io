England beat Ireland 7-6 in a thrilling men's open final at the Parc Omnisports in Vichy on
Saturday. In the process Vaughn Meredith won a fifth consecutive opens division gold medal.
Invicta's Gus Lister was also part of the England triumph as an analyst supporting the team.

Suz Wilson won the women's 40s division for a second time as England beat Scotland 8-0 in the
final.

In the men's 55s, Adrian Riggs and Martin Reynard starred in the England team that defeated Wales
4-1 to claim gold. Martin, who was a late addition to the squad having originally been named as a
reserve, was named player of the final.

In the mixed open division, after a series of impressive wins took them to the final, Lydia
Ticehurst and England had to settle for the silver medal as hosts France ran out 9-6 winners.

It was silver also for Dan Dyer and the England men's 45s &mdash; with the French victorious
in the final after the two teams had traded victories in the pool stage &mdash; while
Eddie French won a bronze medal as assistant coach of the England men's 50s side.
