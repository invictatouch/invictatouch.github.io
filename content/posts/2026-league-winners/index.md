Summer League veterans the Wombles headed into this year's competition aiming for a third
consecutive title but their hopes were ended at the semi-final stage last week by the youthful
skills of the Flankers.
Flankers' reward was a place in the Social League One final against the pre-event
favourites, Tucking Fouchers, who were stacked with high-level touch experience. The final was
extremely close with the teams ultimately separated by just a single score as the newly-formed
Tucking Fouchers won the league at their first attempt with a 9-8 victory.

In the other game at the top end, Touching Turkeys defeated Wombles in the third-place play-off.
In the bottom half of League One, Leg Catchers won the plate final against Ruck Off.

Each week our referees cast their votes for the most valuable male and female players in
each match and these votes were tallied to determine the overall MVPs for the season. Owain Collins
of Leg Catchers was this year's League One male MVP while the female MVP prize was shared between
Erin Collins of Ruck Off and Ella Jenkins from Touching Turkeys.


The Social League Two final was contested between Burgess Hodgson and Scoregasms after the latter's
shock victory over the previously unbeaten Professors in last week's semi-finals. Burgess Hodgson
took early control of the final and ran out 9-4 winners. Their players Will Rayner and Emily
Williams were named the 2026 League Two male and female MVPs.

The Professors took third place with victory over Oil Tankers while Exiles won the League Two plate
final 8-2 against Misfits.
