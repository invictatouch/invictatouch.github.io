While the tournament is primarily intended for nations in the Asia-Pacific region, other countries
have been permitted to enter.
Teams from Australia and New Zealand have long been the benchmark in international touch so
for England, the dominant nation in Europe, this represents an opportunity for its seniors and
masters teams to test themselves against a higher standard of opposition as they build towards the
2028 World Cup in New Zealand.

Fresh from [their triumph at the European Championships in France in
July]({{<relref "/posts/2026-euros-results" >}}), Adrian Riggs and Martin Reynard have once again
been selected in the England men's over-55s squad. They will take on Australia, New Zealand, and
hosts Japan over the four days. The Euros was the first time that this age division had been
contested in capped international competition so their opponents will all be making their first
apperances in the 55s.

The women's over-40s division consists of the top three teams from the 2024 World Cup - champions
Australia, runners-up New Zealand, and bronze medalists &mdash; and European champions &mdash;
England. They will compete in a combined pool with the women's 35s teams before the top two 40s
teams face-off for the gold medal. Invicta's Suz Wilson is again part of the England squad and, with
48 international caps to her name already, will be aiming for a landmark half-century in
Japan.

European silver medalist Dan Dyer is again part of the England men's 45s team as they face world
champions Australia, World Cup runners-up New Zealand, hosts Japan, and Chinese Taipei in their
division.

The Asia-Pacific Seniors Touch Cup begins on Wednesday 7th October at the Tochigi Sports Park in
Utsunomiya and concludes with the medal matches on Saturday 11th October. You can follow the
results and standings on the [Federation of International Touch
website](https://www.internationaltouch.org/events/asia-pacific-seniors-touch-cup/2026/). Selected
matches will be available to watch live (in the early hours of the morning UK time) or on demand on
the [FIT YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@internationaltouch).
