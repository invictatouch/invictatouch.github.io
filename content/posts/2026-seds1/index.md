  Despite being short on numbers, Invicta's mixed development side enjoyed their first run out of
  the winter at round one of the South East Development Series in Horsham on Saturday.
---
Nine clubs from across the region sent teams to West Sussex for the tournament.
In the first pool stage, Invicta went down 4-1 against London Eagles and suffered a close 4-2 defeat
against hosts Horsham Tigers. Those results put the team into the Bowl pool for the second half of
the competition.

The most important game of the day was the Kent derby against our good friends from Tunbridge Wells
Titans. Invicta claimed the bragging rights for East Kent with a comfortable 8-2 victory over the
team from the West. That meant that our final game of the day, against Brighton Seagulls, would
decide the Bowl winners. A competitive match finished 7-5 in favour of the Sussex side to leave
Invicta eighth overall but with plenty of encouragement for the remainder of the series.

All eleven Invicta players acquitted themselves well under the guidance of player-coach Matt Mayall
but a special mention is required for Ellen, Katie and Devon who, as the only three female players
to travel to Horsham, had to put in a massive shift for the team.

---
_Round 2 of the South East Development Series will take place in Horsham on Saturday 5th December._
