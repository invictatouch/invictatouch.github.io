Australia has the strongest domestic competitions in the world and England Touch is sending its
men's and women's opens teams to take part in the two biggest events as they continue to build
towards the 2028 World Cup in New Zealand. First up is the
[Queensland State Cup](https://qldstatecup.qldtouch.com.au) on the Sunshine Coast from 27th to 29th
November. Then the following week they head south to Port Macquarie to play in the
[New South Wales State Cup](https://nswtouch.com.au/representative/state-cup/) between 4th and 6th
December.

After winning his fifth consecutive Euros gold medal in France in July, Vaughn has once again been
selected in the men's open squad for the Australia tour.
