summary:
  Four of our younger players were selected to play for the South East Sharks and South East Taipans
  at the Junior Nationals in Nottingham last weekend.
---
Canterbury Summer League MVP Erin Collins played in the Sharks side that reached the Girls'
under-17s final with a drop-off victory over the South West Saxons in the semi-final.
The Sharks eventually finished as runners-up to the Midlands Tigers.

Will Sands featured for the Taipans Boys' under-17s. After losing narrowly in the cup
quarter-final the Taipans went on to win the second-tier plate competition with a victory over
the West Wildcats in the final.

Poppy Sands was part of the Sharks under-14 girls team that finished fourth while younger sister
Betsy played for the under-12s Taipans girls team that finished sixth.
